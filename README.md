# Networking

A working record of my networking journey — labs, configurations, topologies, and notes built through practice and repetition.

This repository is where I document what I build, break, and rebuild as I learn networking. It is not tied to a single platform, vendor, certification, or toolset. It grows with me as my focus shifts and my understanding deepens.

---

## Purpose

The goal of this repo is simple: turn practice into evidence.

Certifications prove I studied. This repo proves I configured, tested, troubleshot, and understood. It is a place for lab notes, topology designs, command references, and lessons learned from real hands-on work.

---

## Philosophy

- Learn by configuring, not just reading.
- Document the "why" behind every design choice.
- Break things on purpose, then fix them.
- Understand the protocol, not just the command.
- Stay consistent. One lab at a time.

---

## What You'll Find Here

Over time, this repo will contain a growing collection of networking work. Topics may include:

- Lab topologies and configurations
- Routing and switching fundamentals
- Network design and segmentation
- Security hardening and access control
- Troubleshooting scenarios and resolutions
- Command references and protocol notes

The scope will expand as my skills and interests evolve. What stays constant is the intent: clean, honest, useful documentation of real work.

---

## How This Repo Is Organized

Structure may change as the repo grows. Folders will be added, renamed, or reorganized as needed. The goal is not a fixed layout — it is clarity and discoverability over time.

## Who This Is For

- **Me** — as a reference, a revision tool, and a record of progress.
